HOW TO PLAY

Move: use the arrow pad (or arrow keys / WASD).

Interact: stand next to a person or object, face it and click button A. Walking into people no longer starts a chat.

Battle: step into tall grass to enter the bush field, then walk up to an evil Quizmon and choose Fight. Beat the clock: Easy 3 min, Medium 2 min, Hard 1 min. Answer within 15 seconds for a combo and a 50% chance of a critical hit (+20% damage).

Level up: win battles for XP. Each level adds HP and damage.

Coins & shops: beating a Quizmon earns 3 / 5 / 8 coins (Easy / Medium / Hard). Spend them on potions with Jammy (stall north of the road), outfits with Edward (the tailor shop, second house on the north side), haircuts with Jemar (barber hut, north side of the main road) or new Quizmons in your Bag.

Apugan cave: enter the stone gate in the north. Beat every evil Quizmon in a stage to unlock the stairs. 7 stages, the last one ruled by the crowned King. Each kill earns coins, the King gives 20. If you faint, the whole Apugan cave restarts!

Heal: rest on your bed or ask Mama (inside your house) for pandesal.

Goal arrow: the gold Goal bar says what to do, and an arrow with a gold trail shows where to go and how many steps are left. It guides you through the tutorial too.

Quests: villagers with a gold ! have a quest; a green ? means turn it in. Tap the Map button to see where they are (tap a face on the map for details).

Battles: answers are your moves. Right answers make Pixelo attack; wrong answers let the Quizmon strike.

Settings: tap the gear icon in game for difficulty, display mode (2DS or fullscreen), button side, HUD size and more. Tap the corner-brackets icon to toggle fullscreen.

Navigate: open Quests, pick NPC Quests, and tap Navigate to get a gold trail and arrow. You can cancel a quest there too.

Account: log in with your player name, grade and section. Your progress saves automatically and loads next time you log in. Use a transfer code to move your save to another device.

Saved games: open Saved games on the main menu to keep up to 10 separate saves, like worlds. Each shows its level, stage, mode and more. You can load, rename, copy or delete them, and New game always makes a new save instead of overwriting your old one.
